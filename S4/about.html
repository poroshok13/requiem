About REQUIEM

More than just a PC club — an esports arena and lounge built for competitive gaming and collegiate community.

Next-Generation Gaming Environment

REQUIEM was founded to solve a major problem for local collegiate players: the prohibitive cost of flagship 360Hz esports hardware and unstable residential Internet connections.

We built a modern, sound-isolated center equipped with industrial air ventilation, dedicated 10 Gbps fiber routing with redundant failover, and ergonomic Secretlab seating.
