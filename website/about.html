About and contact

An esports hall and lounge for competitive players and the student community of Astana.

StoryTeamGalleryContactCredits

Flagship hardware, without the flagship price

Requiem was founded to solve two problems for local student players: the cost of 360Hz esports hardware and unstable home internet.

We built a sound-isolated hall with industrial ventilation, a redundant 10 Gbps fiber line and ergonomic Secretlab seating.
