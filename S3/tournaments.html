Tournament Regulations

Rules and requirements for participating squads.

All players must compete on verified personal accounts. External macros, software assists, or hardware exploitation result in immediate team disqualification and permanent club ban.

Teams are provided with a 30-minute warmup buffer before match starts to plug in personal mice, keyboards, and calibrate settings on 360Hz monitors.

Cash rewards are awarded directly to squad captains on tournament conclusion day via verified bank wire or cash settlement without platform commission.
